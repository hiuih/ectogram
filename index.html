What changes

A browser tab, minus the browser.

01

Real dock presence

Its own icon, its own app name, its own Cmd+Tab slot — not buried in forty browser tabs.

02

Camera & mic that work

Proper hardened-runtime entitlements, so macOS actually prompts you for access — for Stories, Reels, and video calls.

03

Native notifications

Alerts land in Notification Center like any other Mac app — no more missed activity because a tab was backgrounded.

04

Stays logged in

Session persists across restarts. Open it, you're in — same as the last time you closed it.

05

Native title bar

No more custom on-page controls fighting with real macOS window chrome — a genuine title bar, always.

06

It's the real thing

Every photo, DM, and login goes straight to Instagram's own servers. Nothing is proxied, logged, or altered.
